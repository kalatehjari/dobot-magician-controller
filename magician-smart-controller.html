Picture to lines

Threshold decides what counts as ink (darker than the threshold). Detail drops specks and small wiggles at low settings and keeps fine features at high ones. Curves at 0 keeps every corner sharp; higher values round off corners and wobbles, keeping only sharp spikes.

Where on the paper

Green = where the pen can draw (from Auto-calibrate). Drag the drawing to move it, a corner square to resize it, the round knob to turn it — every line stays inside the green zone (the dashed box is only the handle and may stick out). With the map selected: arrow keys move it 1 mm (Shift: 10 mm), + and − resize, [ and ] turn. Red dot = the pen, live; red lines = what has been drawn on this sheet. Dotted grey = pen-up travel.

Drawing

Load a drawing or a picture to start.
Advanced
Smooth mode sends the pen path as continuous-path (CP) moves, which the Magician blends without stopping, at the pen speed. Pen-up travel is a straight move at the travel speed, so the pen keeps its height. If lines look wobbly, lower the pen speed or the acceleration.

Record takes

While recording, the arm's real position is sampled about 7 times a second. Move it with the Mouse tab, arrow keys or Z slider. Each recording becomes a take.

Learn a repeated movement

Takes are time-aligned by distance travelled and averaged. Blue line = learned path. Shaded circles = spread between takes, so you can see where your repetitions disagree.

Dance

Moves

Songs are analysed on this Mac: tempo, beats and loudness. Quiet passages get calm moves (sway, nod, wave, circle); loud ones get energetic moves (robot, pump, disco, head bang). Every move is a keyframe on a beat; if the arm lands late, raise the latency compensation. A different seed gives a different dance to the same song.

Imitate me

Stopped.
Make a fist for half a second to freeze or unfreeze the arm. If your hand or arm leaves the view, the robot holds still. Smaller “hand range” = bigger robot moves for the same hand movement. Esc stops everything.

Air drawing

0 strokes
Pinch thumb and index finger to put the pen down; open them to lift it. Strokes land in the Plotter's drawing area with the same orientation as its preview, then get the same path optimisation.

Teach by showing

After a 3-second countdown your hand path is recorded (up to 30 s) and saved as a take in Record & learn. Show the same movement a few times, then use “Learn from selected takes” to average them.

Face reading is off.

Behaviour

Press “Capture my neutral face” once while relaxed: expressions are then measured against your own resting face, which makes smiles and frowns far more reliable. Reactions play around the arm's current position and always return to it.

Connection

Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge — they support USB serial. Safari and Firefox cannot connect to the robot. Close DobotStudio or any other program that is using the arm's serial port.

Safety limits and calibration

These defaults are conservative starting values, not measurements from your unit. Before drawing: set the min/max reach and Z range to match your calibration, lower the tip by hand (Mouse tab, Z slider) until it just touches the paper, then press Set pen-down = current Z.
Not calibrated yet.
Auto-calibrate homes the arm, checks its joint readings against the Magician's geometry (rear arm 135 mm, forearm 147 mm, joint limits) and computes where it can reach at every height; you only lower the pen onto the paper. Manual calibration jogs the arm to its ends instead. With auto-calibration, every move is checked against the real reach at its height; min/max reach here set the area the smart modes use.

Speeds (mm/s)

Settings are remembered in this browser. Drawing speed is slower than live follow for cleaner lines.

Vision models

The camera features use Google MediaPipe 0.10.35 (pinned on purpose: newer 1.x builds send usage metrics with no opt-out). The library comes from jsDelivr and the face, hand and body models from Google the first time a camera feature starts. For offline use, download face_landmarker.task, gesture_recognizer.task and pose_landmarker_lite.task once and pick them here.
