Magician Smart Controller

Software for the Dobot Magician robot arm that runs in your browser. Plug the arm in by USB and draw, write, shade, 3D print, move, record, dance and react — straight from Chrome or Edge, with nothing to install.

Open the app Download one HTML file, 4.3 MB

For Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS computers. No arm at hand? The built-in simulator runs every feature.

What it does

One page holds everything: a pen plotter with a big drawing library, text and shading, a 3D-printing slicer, and ways to move the arm by hand, by music or by camera.

Pen plotter for SVGs and pictures

Draw SVG files, or turn pictures (PNG, JPG, WEBP, GIF, BMP) into lines: outlines for shapes, single centre lines for line art and lettering, or edges for photos. Threshold, detail and smoothing are adjustable, and the lines can be saved as SVG.

Set the size in millimetres, turn, mirror and drag the drawing on a map of the paper. The green zone shows where the pen can reach, and every line is kept inside it. Strokes are ordered to cut pen-up travel.

2,245 ready-made drawings in 31 categories

Animals, cars, poses, faces, flags in colour, city skylines, songs as sheet music, constellations and the zodiac, leaves, topography, aircraft, sci-fi, clothing and shoes, signs, emojis, technology, music, art and more.

Search or pick a category, click a drawing to add it to the sheet, or download it as SVG. The whole library also comes as a folder of SVG files with each release.

Text, shading and several objects on one sheet

  • Text in 13 single-line fonts (Hershey and EMS) with up to four styles: letter height in millimetres, rotation, alignment, spacing, several lines and accented letters.
  • Persian text (فارسی), written right to left in a single-line Persian font: letters join by themselves, vowel marks sit on their letters, and English words and numbers inside stay left to right.
  • Shading fills closed shapes with hatch lines, cross-hatch or three directions, at any angle and spacing, with closer lines for darker colours.
  • Several objects: drawings, pictures and text each keep their own size, place, turn and shading. Save and open layouts, and export the sheet as one SVG.

Smooth, quiet drawing you can watch

The whole drawing runs as one continuous path with low, rounded pen hops instead of stopping at every lift. Choose Quiet & smooth, Balanced or Fast, or let the quiet-speed finder listen through the microphone and pick the quietest speeds.

Before drawing, Hover round traces the outside just above the paper so you can check its position. While the arm draws, the pen shows as a red dot and finished lines turn red. Pause, resume or stop at any moment.

3D printing from STL files

With Dobot's 3D-printing kit, load STL or OBJ files or one of seven built-in models, place them on the Ø80 mm plate and slice them in the page: walls, solid top and bottom, infill, supports, skirt or brim, and vase mode. Dobot's recommended settings are the defaults.

Preview every line in 3D or layer by layer, then print straight over USB with line numbers, checksums and automatic resend, live progress, pause and stop — or download the G-code for Repetier Host or Cura.

Also in the app

  • Mouse control

    Click or drag on a map of the reach to move the arm, nudge it with the keyboard, and work the pen, suction cup and gripper.

  • Record & learn

    Record movements, average several takes into one smooth learned movement, and replay it.

  • Dance

    Finds the beat in a song file, from the microphone or a metronome, and dances to it.

  • Imitate

    The arm mirrors your hand or arm through the webcam. Draw in the air with a pinch, or teach a movement by showing it.

  • Face & reactions

    Reads expressions, winks, nods, head shakes and hand signs, reacts to them, and can follow your face.

  • Auto-calibration

    Centres the arm, checks its joint readings, measures the tool offset and works out the reachable area. You only lower the pen onto the paper.

  • Safe drawing zone

    Click the corners of your paper on the map. Drawings, pen-up travel and redraws stay inside it; save the pen height for each kind of paper.

  • Safety lock

    After centring, every movement is checked before it starts. If it would leave the allowed area, adjust it or let the app trim it to the boundary.

  • Bluetooth & Wi-Fi

    Leave the cable: use Dobot's Bluetooth module in Chrome or Edge, or the Wi-Fi module through a small bridge program.

  • Simulator

    Try every feature without hardware, including 3D printing on a simulated printer.

  • Safety first

    Every target is kept inside the reachable space, alarms are shown and can be cleared, and STOP (or Esc) halts everything.

Get started

  1. Open the app in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer — online, or download the single HTML file from the latest release and open it from your disk.
  2. Switch the Magician on and plug it in with USB. Click Connect USB and pick the arm's serial port.
  3. In the calibration dialog, click Start auto-calibration, lower the pen onto the paper with the ▼ buttons, then click This is the paper.
  4. Open Plotter, pick a drawing from the library, drop in an SVG or a picture, or type some text. Place it in the green zone, press Hover round to check the paper, and click Draw.

No arm at hand? Click Simulator instead of Connect USB. If the arm is not in the port list, check the cable and that the arm is on; some computers need a driver for the arm's USB-serial chip.

3D printing with Dobot's kit

  1. Fit the 3D-printing kit as in Dobot's guide: hot end on the forearm, heater on interface 4, fan on 5, temperature sensor on 6, extruder motor on Stepper1.
  2. Put Dobot's 3D-printer firmware on the arm: in DobotStudio choose Tools → Open 3D Printing. Then close DobotStudio and Repetier Host.
  3. In the 3D print tab, click Connect 3D printer (USB), press Heat, wait for 200 °C and Feed 10 mm until plastic comes out.
  4. Tape the plate, lower the nozzle onto the tape (hold the forearm's unlock button) and press the Key button on the base, or Save nozzle height. The arm beeps.
  5. Load a model, press Slice, check the preview and press Start print…

To draw again, the arm needs DobotStudio's normal firmware; DobotStudio offers to switch back when you next connect with it.

Safety

  • Keep hands and objects clear of the arm. In the Dance, Imitate and Face tabs the arm moves by itself.
  • STOP (or Esc) halts motion and clears the arm's command queue. If the arm's red light comes on, clear the alarm with the red bar.
  • When 3D printing, the nozzle reaches 200 °C or more. Don't touch it, and stay with the printer.
  • The software limits help, but they are not a substitute for watching the arm.

Requirements and privacy

  • A computer with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Safari and Firefox don't support Web Serial, so they can't connect to the arm.
  • The camera features need a webcam, and an internet connection the first time to download Google's MediaPipe vision models.
  • Everything runs in your browser. Camera images stay on your computer, and settings are kept in your browser's storage. This website has no cookies and no analytics.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the app in Chrome or Edge, online or as the downloaded HTML file. For 3D printing the arm needs Dobot's 3D-printer firmware, which DobotStudio puts on it. Some computers also need a driver for the arm's USB-serial chip.

Which browsers and computers does it work on?

Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS computer. The page talks to the arm through Web Serial, which Safari and Firefox do not support, so they cannot connect; phones and tablets cannot either.

Can I connect without the USB cable?

Yes, with Dobot's wireless modules. The Bluetooth module (Wireless 1) connects from Chrome or Edge with Web Bluetooth. The Wi-Fi module (Wireless 2) is told your network once over USB from the app; after that a small bridge program on your computer (Python, nothing to install, downloadable from the app) passes the commands on, because web pages cannot send the module's UDP messages.

Can I try it without a robot arm?

Yes. Click Simulator instead of Connect USB and every feature runs on a simulated arm; the 3D print tab has its own printer simulator.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Download magician-smart-controller.html from the latest release and open it from your disk. Only the camera features need the internet, to download Google's MediaPipe vision models the first time you use them.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The app runs in your browser. Camera images are processed on your computer and are not uploaded, and calibration and settings are kept in your browser's storage. This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

Do my settings carry over between the website and the downloaded file?

No. Browsers keep settings separately for each site, so the online app and a downloaded file each keep their own calibration and settings. Calibrate once in each.

How do I switch between 3D printing and drawing?

3D printing uses Dobot's 3D-printer firmware: in DobotStudio choose Tools → Open 3D Printing. For drawing and the other tabs the arm needs DobotStudio's normal firmware again; DobotStudio offers to switch back when you next connect with it.

Has it been tested on a real arm?

It is tested with the built-in arm and printer simulators and in Chrome on macOS. Testing on a physical arm is limited so far, and 3D printing has not yet been tried on a physical 3D-printing kit, so watch the first drawings and prints closely and report problems on GitHub.

Is this an official Dobot product?

No. It is an independent open-source project under the MIT licence and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Dobot.
