Picture to lines

Threshold decides what counts as ink (darker than the threshold). Detail drops specks and small wiggles at low settings and keeps fine features at high ones. Curves at 0 keeps every corner sharp; higher values round off corners and wobbles, keeping only sharp spikes.

Where on the paper

Green = where the pen can draw (from Auto-calibrate). Everything you load, pick from the library or type is added to the paper as its own object. Click one to select it, then drag it to move it, drag a corner square to resize it or the round knob to turn it — the size, rotation and shading controls act on the selected object. Every line stays inside the green zone (the dashed box is only the handle and may stick out). Untick an object in the list to leave it out of the drawing. With the map selected: arrow keys move it 1 mm (Shift: 10 mm), + and − resize, [ and ] turn, D copies it, Delete removes it. Red dot = the pen, live; red lines = what has been drawn on this sheet. Dotted grey = pen-up travel.

Text

Single-line fonts: every letter is drawn with one pen line, as if written, so text plots quickly and cleanly. Letter height is the height of a capital letter. Several lines are fine — press Return in the text box. Accented letters missing from a font (ā, ł, ő…) are drawn with the accent added.

Shading

Quick angle
Fills closed shapes with parallel pen lines. Angles belong to the drawing (0° = across, 90° = up and down) and turn with it; spacing is in millimetres on the paper at any size. A shape inside another is a hole in it, so each region is shaded on its own. Click a shape on the map to give it its own shading: each click turns its lines another 45°, then cross-hatches it, then leaves it empty, then returns it to the settings above. Filled shapes in the SVG follows the file's colours: shapes on top hide the ones below and white stays white. Zig-zags follow the outline between lines, so the pen lifts far less.

Drawing

Load a drawing or a picture to start.
Hover round checks the paper before drawing: the pen goes once round the outside of everything that will be drawn, about 5 mm above the paper, without touching it — box goes round the corners of a rectangle that holds it all, outline follows the outside of the lines. Its path shows dashed on the map.
Advanced
Smooth mode sends the whole drawing as one continuous path (CP moves), which the Magician blends without stopping: lines are cut into short even pieces, and the pen hops between nearby strokes on low rounded arcs (higher for longer hops, never lower than "Lowest pen hop"). Only jumps longer than the hop limit lift fully and travel at the travel speed. If lines look wobbly, lower the pen speed or the acceleration; if the pen catches the paper between strokes, raise the lowest hop.

Dance

Moves

Songs are analysed on this Mac: tempo, beats and loudness. Quiet passages get calm moves (sway, nod, wave, circle); loud ones get energetic moves (robot, pump, disco, head bang). Every move is a keyframe on a beat; if the arm lands late, raise the latency compensation. A different seed gives a different dance to the same song.

Imitate me

Stopped.
Make a fist for half a second to freeze or unfreeze the arm. If your hand or arm leaves the view, the robot holds still. Smaller “hand range” = bigger robot moves for the same hand movement. Esc stops everything.

Air drawing

0 strokes
Pinch thumb and index finger to put the pen down; open them to lift it. Strokes land in the Plotter's drawing area with the same orientation as its preview, then get the same path optimisation.

Teach by showing

After a 3-second countdown your hand path is recorded (up to 30 s) and saved as a take in Record & learn. Show the same movement a few times, then use “Learn from selected takes” to average them.

Behaviour

Press “Capture my neutral face” once while relaxed: expressions are then measured against your own resting face, which makes smiles and frowns far more reliable. Reactions play around the arm's current position and always return to it.

Connection

Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge — they support USB serial. Safari and Firefox cannot connect to the robot. Close DobotStudio or any other program that is using the arm's serial port.

Safety limits and calibration

These defaults are conservative starting values, not measurements from your unit. Before drawing: set the min/max reach and Z range to match your calibration, lower the tip by hand (Mouse tab, Z slider) until it just touches the paper, then press Set pen-down = current Z.
Not calibrated yet.
Auto-calibrate homes the arm, checks its joint readings against the Magician's geometry (rear arm 135 mm, forearm 147 mm, joint limits) and computes where it can reach at every height; you only lower the pen onto the paper. Manual calibration jogs the arm to its ends instead. With auto-calibration, every move is checked against the real reach at its height; min/max reach here set the area the smart modes use.

Smooth & quiet motion

Stepper motors are loudest when they speed up or stop hard, start and stop over and over, or run at a speed where they resonate. Quiet & smooth uses gentle acceleration and lower speeds; drawings run as one continuous path with low, rounded pen hops instead of stopping at every lift; and following glides along a smoothed path instead of jumping from point to point. Choosing a feel sets the speeds below, which you can still fine-tune. If gliding ever feels laggy or odd on your arm, switch Following to Steps.
